So what: invest + build

Equity lens

Long physics: turbines (GE 116GW, Siemens 69GW backlog), transformers, HBM (SK/Samsung/Micron sold out '26), substrates (Ibiden/Unimicron), spot brokers in glut.
Neutral labs: rent without utilization is a short. Watch 70% utilization + on-demand cuts >20%.
History rhyme: Nortel/Cisco vendor financing → write-offs; fiber survived cheap. GPUs rot faster — distressed compute funds open weights.

Product lens

WorldTrainer: price by verified shape (multi-tool fix + test pass), not tokens. Weekly pools, estimate-before-upload, per-repo controls.
Chief-of-staff: stay hybrid — local VAD/router (~300ms manners), frontier brains. Voice collapsed to ~$0.07 all-in/min.
Watch weekly: H100 spot floor, GB200 listing, on-demand cuts, energization dates (Tydal Dec-26, Beacon Q1-27, Justified H2-27).
